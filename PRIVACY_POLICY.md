# Privacy Policy for తత్త్వమసి - Ayyappa Nitya Pooja

**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

**Application Name:** తత్త్వమసి - Ayyappa Nitya Pooja

**Developer:** Tatvamasi Devotional Team  

---

### 1. Introduction
"తత్త్వమసి - Ayyappa Nitya Pooja" is a sacred devotional application created to guide devotees of Lord Ayyappa Swamy in daily rituals, pooja procedures, bhajans, japa meditation, and pilgrimage guidelines. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
* **No Location Tracking:** We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.
* **No Financial Information:** Our app is 100% free and does not require financial details or payments.
* **No Advertising Identifiers (AdID):** Our application contains no third-party advertisements, behavioral trackers, or analytics SDKs that track users across apps.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage on Device
To provide a smooth, offline-first devotional experience, the app stores minimal preferences directly on your device using standard local storage:
* **Language Preference:** Stored locally so the app remembers your preferred language (Telugu or English).
* **Japa Mala Counter:** Stored locally so your chanting count is saved on your device.

This information is kept strictly on your local device and is never transmitted to any external server.

---

### 4. Audio & Network Access
* **Offline Devotional Guide:** All text, pooja steps, mantras, and lyrics function 100% offline without requiring internet access.
* **Audio Streaming:** When you choose to listen to optional devotional audio or suprabhatham, network access (`android.permission.INTERNET`) is used solely to stream the requested audio content or access official devotional sources. No user identifiers are logged.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)
Our application is safe for families and users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 6. Security
Since we do not collect or store personal information on remote servers, your privacy is safeguarded by design.
